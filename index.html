About the volume

The volume brings together interdisciplinary social science approaches to public administration, with a strong emphasis on sociological perspectives that have been less present in the existing literature. Its design deliberately sets cases from lower-income countries in the Global South alongside cases from wealthy Western nations.

Contributors were assembled through a mix of invited authors and an open call for new work. This first workshop is where chapter drafts get close, collegial feedback, and where the four thematic clusters begin to talk to one another.

Four thematic clusters

Cluster 1

Boundaries, Delegation & Coproduction

Where the state ends, who acts for it, and how public work is shared.

Cluster 2

Burdens & Inequality

What administration asks of people, and who bears the cost.

Cluster 3

Professional Careers & Bureaucratic Sensemaking

How administrators build careers and make sense of their work.

Cluster 4

Corruption & Capacity

What explains state performance, and its failures.

Travel

Getting to South Bend

You can fly directly into South Bend, or into Chicago's O'Hare (ORD) or Midway (MDW) and continue east by train, bus or car.

Confirm before you book. Train and bus schedules change by season and day of the week. Check each provider's timetable for Friday, Dec. 11 and Sunday, Dec. 13. Sunday follows the weekend schedule, and December-specific timetables may not be posted yet, so use the links below and recheck closer to your trip.

Fly into South Bend (SBN)

South Bend International Airport (code SBN) is about 15 minutes by car from campus. Book flights to South Bend, Indiana. Rideshare (Uber, Lyft), taxis and rental cars are available at the airport.

South Bend International Airport

Fly into Chicago (ORD or MDW)

O'Hare is about two hours from campus by car and Midway about ninety minutes, depending on traffic. Without a car, you have three main options.

OptionHow it works
South Shore Line trainTrains run from Millennium Station in downtown Chicago directly to the South Bend International Airport station, a trip of roughly three hours. From O'Hare take the CTA Blue Line downtown; from Midway take the Orange Line. Weekend timetables are different from weekday ones, so check the current schedule. A new airport station is planned for 2026–27, so confirm the stop and trip details before you travel.
GreyhoundBuses run between downtown Chicago and South Bend. See greyhound.com for schedules.
Rental carRental counters are at SBN and at both Chicago airports. The drive is straightforward but can be slow in winter weather.

By Amtrak

Amtrak serves South Bend (station code SOB) from Chicago and the East Coast, and Niles, Michigan, a short ride north of South Bend. Confirm timetables for your dates.

Driving

Notre Dame is on the north side of South Bend. See Getting around for parking, and ask your hotel about parking once it is confirmed.

Heading home on Sunday

The workshop ends at 1:45 pm ET, and the airport is close, so you can be there by about 2:05 pm. Flights leaving South Bend after about 4:00 pm work well, and a 3:30 pm departure is possible but tight. If you plan to take the South Shore Line, the current Sunday timetable lists departures from the South Bend airport station at 1:14 pm and 5:47 pm. The 1:14 would mean leaving the workshop early, while the 5:47 works without leaving early. These are the current published times; a December-specific timetable is not yet available, so please follow the link and recheck closer to your trip. If you are returning through Chicago, remember the one-hour time change.

Travel

Getting around

Most of what you need is close to the workshop building, and most people will not need a car.

On foot

The workshop is in 4060 Jenkins Nanovic Halls (1399 N. Notre Dame Avenue). We will post walking directions once the hotel is confirmed. On workshop mornings we plan to walk over together as a group, so dress for December in northern Indiana.

Rideshare and taxis

Uber, Lyft and Rides2U operate in South Bend, and taxis are available at the airport. Rideshare is the easiest way to reach the airport, the train station or downtown.

Local buses and campus shuttle

Transpo Route 7
Runs from the Hesburgh Library circle to South Bend. It is free for people with a Notre Dame ID; otherwise a standard fare applies.
Transpo Route 4
Connects the South Bend bus station with South Bend International Airport and the Washington Street Amtrak station.
Campus shuttle
Notre Dame and Saint Mary's have a campus route that serves Eddy Street Commons and the Hesburgh Library circle during the academic year. Confirm its hours for the weekend of Dec. 12–13 on the Transpo website.

Parking

  • Hotel parking, if any, will be described on the Where to stay page once the hotel is set.
  • If you drive to campus, visitor parking rules vary by lot and event. Notre Dame Transportation Services can confirm the best option for the weekend: (574) 631-6467 or undtrans@nd.edu.
